Before You Visit

What time are the services?

Sunday Service runs 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM and Thursday's Wonders & Deliverance Night runs 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

Who can attend?

Everyone is welcome — children, young people, families and seniors. Come as you are.

How can I request prayer?

Use our contact form and choose “Prayer Request”, or email info@lifegraceministries.com.
