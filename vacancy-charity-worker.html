Charity Worker

A Charity Worker supports the ministry's humanitarian and social outreach programmes and helps deliver practical assistance to individuals and families in need. They help ensure LifeGrace Ministries fulfils its charitable mission.

⚲ Bromley / CroydonFlexible HoursTemporary

Safeguarding

LifeGrace Ministries is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of all. All appointments are subject to appropriate checks in line with our safeguarding and confidentiality policies.

Core Responsibilities

Community Support & Welfare

  • Deliver practical support to individuals and families in need.
  • Identify vulnerable individuals and connect them with ministry resources.
  • Participate in food banks, clothing drives, and outreach activities.

Administration & Compliance

  • Maintain records of beneficiaries, donations, and programme activities.
  • Support fundraising campaigns and grant applications.
  • Ensure compliance with charity regulations and safeguarding policies.

Desirable Attributes

  • Previous experience in charity coordination or community outreach.
  • Good administrative and organisational skills.
  • A compassionate, patient, and service-driven personality.
  • Knowledge of local community needs within South London.

Working Conditions

  • Flexible working hours including evenings and weekends.
  • Community-based work across Bromley, Croydon, and surrounding areas.
  • Occasional travel for outreach, conferences, or ministry events.
How to Apply

Apply for this Role

Interested candidates should submit:

  • A CV via info@lifegraceministries.com
  • A brief personal statement outlining their faith journey and motivation for applying
  • Two references (one professional and community-based)
