Temporary Religious Worker

A Religious Worker supports the ministry's spiritual, cultural, and community activities and assists pastors with religious programmes and the ministry's daily operations. They assist pastors, help deliver religious programmes, and contribute to daily operations.

⚲ South LondonFlexible HoursTemporary

Safeguarding

LifeGrace Ministries is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of all. All appointments are subject to appropriate checks in line with our safeguarding and confidentiality policies.

Core Responsibilities

Faith-Based Activities

  • Assist with worship services, prayer meetings, and Bible teaching.
  • Support religious education for children, youth, and adults.
  • Help prepare materials for sermons, classes, and ministry events.

Community & Cultural Engagement

  • Participate in outreach, evangelism, and community visits.
  • Promote positive relationships within the congregation and community.
  • Support cultural celebrations, retreats, and special events.

Operational Support

  • Help organise events, workshops, and ministry programmes.
  • Assist with newsletters, social media, and announcements.
  • Maintain ministry spaces, resources, and equipment.

Pastoral Assistance

  • Provide basic emotional and spiritual support under pastoral supervision.
  • Help identify community needs and support follow-up actions.

Desirable Attributes

  • Previous experience in church leadership or religious activities.
  • Ability to assist with worship, teaching, or music ministry.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • A compassionate, patient, and faith-driven personality.

Working Conditions

  • Flexible working hours including evenings and weekends.
  • Community-based work across Bromley, Croydon, and surrounding areas.
  • Occasional travel for outreach, conferences, or ministry events.
How to Apply

Apply for this Role

Interested candidates should submit:

  • A CV via info@lifegraceministries.com
  • A brief personal statement outlining their faith journey and motivation for applying
  • Two references (one pastoral and community-based)
